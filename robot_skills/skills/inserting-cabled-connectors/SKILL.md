# Insert, reobserve, and release a cabled plug

Replan around mate_plug_point, mate_mouth_point, and mate_bore_axis. Move back, ease grip, push, restore grip, and select the matching perception body for one reseat. Replan the second push from that new observation.

If the reseat view is lost, release and retreat rather than inventing an alignment. The released exit means commands completed. The independent settled evaluator decides whether the plug stayed inserted; the workflow has no visual success-claim node.

Before aligning to a tilted held bore, establish enough axial clearance for the tab, then advance along the measured bore axis. Do not lower the tab into the mouth during transverse alignment.

Bound each entry and axial retry by the full hand geometry, maintaining 3 mm clearance from the measured mouth plane. Preload cannot override this bound. Release upward outside that plane, including when the free receptacle is pitched.

Compose the example subgraph using its declared inputs and route every exit, including `on_error`. A normal exit describes the node's observation or completed command; only the separate settled task evaluator determines benchmark success.

Use RGB-D observations and robot proprioception for the evaluated vision path. Oracle recipes and privileged survey probes are for diagnostics; the vision path must work with those tools withheld (`--perception vision --no-privileged`).

Grippers act through commanded joints and contact friction. Do not introduce object teleports, grasp welds, cable latches, retention forces, or suppressed fixture collisions. The physical cable attachment and fixed bench anchor belong to the scene.

These recipes require the RoboSimStudio connector; they are not a claim of hardware portability. SAM3 calls require the deployed segmentation tool and its weights.

# Brace a free bulkhead with contact

Route fixed receptacles to bare. For a free bulkhead, plan the support hand from scene_barrel_point, open, stage, seat, close with settling time, and lift 30 mm to clear the bench.

Braced means the contact-grasp sequence completed. It does not create a constraint or guarantee that friction will resist the later insertion load.

Compose the example subgraph using its declared inputs and route every exit, including `on_error`. A normal exit describes the node's observation or completed command; only the separate settled task evaluator determines benchmark success.

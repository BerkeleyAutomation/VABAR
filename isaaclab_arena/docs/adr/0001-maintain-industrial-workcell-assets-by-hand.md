# Maintain industrial workcell assets by hand

The industrial FR3 and YAM workcells will maintain their table, sorting-box, pegboard, tool-holder, USB-C fixture, and cable-routing fixture assets as package-owned, self-contained hand-authored compositions. Task-specific fixtures such as trays and sharps containers are declared explicitly in task YAML. We are deliberately not extending or rerunning the historical BDDL converter because these workcells are now curated benchmark definitions, and future layout changes must remain explicit and reviewable in their assets and task YAML.

The Canonical Table is the primary layout anchor. Immovable fixtures are kinematic rigid bodies placed at exact world coordinates and orientations declared in YAML. Exact assembled fixtures are also layout anchors where intentional fixture intersections would otherwise make generic no-overlap placement reject the authored assembly. Table support relations remain explicit where they describe a solver-supported layout, such as the sorting bins. The migration preserves each fixture's existing collision representation rather than redesigning collision geometry. Retired generated backgrounds and converter-only debug geometry are removed after references have been migrated.

FR3 and YAM each have one thin shared table background that references the same Canonical Table and owns that embodiment's single canonical table transform. The shared table closure continues to reference NVIDIA's official Isaac Sim 6.0 asset; the self-contained requirement applies to benchmark-owned fixtures and prevents them from depending on retired generated backgrounds.

Tool-sorting tasks share one physical bin geometry and its existing collision representation, with explicit visual variants preserving task-specific appearances and labels. Cable routing gains a declarative YAML Task Layout for its table, board, and pegs, while its articulated cable and specialized physics construction remain in Python.

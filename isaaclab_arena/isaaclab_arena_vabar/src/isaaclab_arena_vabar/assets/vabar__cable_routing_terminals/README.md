# Cable-routing terminals

The cable anchor and open terminal of the cable routing easy task. Their origin is the tabletop, and local `+X` points the way the cable leaves the fitting.

- `anchor.usda`: two low side rails, an overhead hood, and a saddle at the mouth.
- `port.usda`: two open-top channel walls and a closed back stop.

Both occupy a 32 x 32 mm footprint around a 10 mm channel, with a 12.5 mm terminal rise. Only the boxes collide; the screw heads are visual-only. The fixtures are kinematic with friction 2.0.

Geometry and colours follow Berkeley AUTOLab's procedural cable-route terminals (Apache-2.0).

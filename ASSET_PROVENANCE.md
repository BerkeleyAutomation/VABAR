# Asset provenance

Every object, fixture and robot model VABAR ships, where it came from, and its
licence. Code is Apache-2.0 ([LICENSE](LICENSE)); the robot models keep their
own licences ([NOTICE](NOTICE)). Per-suite detail lives beside the files, in
`tasks/<suite>/assets/PROVENANCE.md`.

> [!WARNING]
> **Resolve before the repository is made public.** Rows marked
> **TODO-before-public** are redistributed here, in a private repository, while
> their licences are confirmed. Each needs either a recorded licence that
> permits redistribution (with attribution added here and in `NOTICE`), or
> replacement or removal of the files.

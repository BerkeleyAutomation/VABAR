## Derived geometry (built in-repo, same licence as its source)

- `syringe/collision_coacd/hull_*.obj` + `manifest.json` — a CoACD approximate
  convex decomposition of `syringe/syringe.obj`, 61 hulls, which every syringe
  in this suite collides on. `scripts/build_coacd_collisions.py` builds these
  and writes the manifest recording its parameters.

  **Why the copy, and why the hulls are committed rather than rebuilt.** The
  mesh is the collection's, shared with `tool_sort`, and a collider is physics:
  this suite had to stop colliding on the export's flange-width box without
  moving `tool_sort`'s syringe underneath it, so it owns its own copy. The hulls
  are the set the reported sweep was measured on. CoACD's MCTS is not
  deterministic across runs even at `seed 0` — the same mesh and the same
  parameters give 58 hulls on one machine against these 61 — so rebuilding them
  would quietly change the physics a number was taken under.

The export these documents stage -- `vab_syringe_packing/tray_pair/` with its sidecar,
the robot-free `scene_norobot.xml` every foreign-robot host stands in, and the
`scene_norobot_box*.xml` copies whose sharps box moved with a layout sweep --
is the collection's, under `assets/vab_tool_sort/`, resolved by path;
see `assets/PROVENANCE.md`.
